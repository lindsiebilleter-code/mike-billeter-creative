Editing

$150/hour, two-hour minimum

For businesses that already have copy they mostly like and want it sharper, tighter, and more distinctly theirs. This isn’t a discount version of writing — it’s a different service for a different situation. If you’ve written your own site and want it to land better, or you have copy from a previous writer that doesn’t quite fit anymore, editing is what you’re looking for.

I’ll review what you have, edit for voice, clarity, and impact, and return a marked-up version with changes and notes. Substantive editing — not just proofreading. If what you actually need is a full rewrite, I’ll tell you that upfront and we’ll talk about scoping it as a writing project instead.

Ghostwriting

$150/hour

LinkedIn posts, longer-form essays, op-eds, executive thought leadership, whatever you need written in your voice rather than mine. Hourly at $150/hour.

Here’s the thing about ghostwriting: a lot of writers can’t actually do it. Their voice is their voice, and whatever they write ends up sounding like them no matter who it’s supposedly for. That’s fine if you’re hiring a writer to sound like a writer. It’s a real problem if you’re paying someone to sound like you.

What I do differently is adjust my style to match whoever I’m writing for — your voice, your audience’s expectations, the platform you’re publishing on. The piece should sound like you wrote it on a particularly sharp day, not like someone else wrote it for you.

I scope ghostwriting hourly because the time it takes depends on how much I’m starting with (bullet points, a voice memo, a half-written draft) and how much voice-capture the piece needs. If you’re considering ghostwriting as an ongoing need, the retainer option is probably a better structure than billing piece by piece.

Retainers are billed at the start of each month. Unused hours don’t roll over (this protects both of us — you don’t accumulate a debt I can’t actually deliver against, and I don’t end up owing a client 20 hours in month six). Either of us can end the arrangement with 30 days notice.

A note on retainers: I take on a small number at a time. The whole point of a retainer is that you get someone who actually knows your business and can deliver consistently, and the math breaks down if I’m trying to maintain that kind of relationship with too many clients at once. So if you reach out and I’m at capacity, I’ll tell you honestly. If I’m available, we’ll talk.

What I don’t do

The flip side of being clear about services is being clear about non-services. A short list, so we don’t end up two emails into a conversation that wasn’t going to work anyway.

Social media management.
I don’t run accounts, schedule posts, or write recurring social content. If you need a single post written occasionally, that can fit under blog or ghostwriting work. But ongoing social management is a different job and you want someone whose actual specialty is that.
Email platform setup and management.
I write the emails. I don’t log into your Mailchimp, set up your automations, manage your list, or troubleshoot deliverability. If you need that side of things handled, I can recommend people who do it well.
Speed-priority work where voice doesn’t matter.
If you need 500 words by tomorrow and you don’t really care how it sounds, AI will do that faster than I will, for free. I write more deliberately because the writing is the point. If the writing isn’t the point, I’m not the right hire.

A few practical notes

Deposits
Projects over $300 require a 50% deposit to start. The remaining 50% is invoiced on final delivery. Smaller projects (one blog post, an editing engagement under two hours) are billed on completion.
Retainers
Billed at the start of each month. Unused hours don’t roll over. Either of us can end the arrangement with 30 days notice.
Revisions
Two rounds included on Website Copy projects, one round on most other projects, additional rounds at $150/hour. A “round” means one consolidated set of feedback, not a series of one-off requests.
Paused or canceled projects
If a project gets paused or canceled after I’ve started, the deposit covers the work done up to that point. Anything beyond that, we handle case by case.
Ownership
Once final payment is received, the copy is yours. You can use it, modify it, republish it, ignore it, whatever you want. I don’t retain any ownership or licensing rights to client work.
