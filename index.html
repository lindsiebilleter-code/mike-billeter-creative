See how this works

Here’s what’s actually happening to your copy.

You know your business is different. You can feel it. The way you talk about it over coffee, the way you describe it to a friend who asks what you do — there’s a specific personality in there, and it’s the reason people choose you over the competition.

Then it hits the website, and somehow it comes out sounding like every other business in your industry.

That’s not a you problem. That’s a writing problem. Most copy gets written the same way it’s always been written: pull the same words and phrases from the same bucket, smooth out anything interesting or different, end up with something that’s technically correct and totally forgettable. AI hasn’t helped — it’s made the bucket bigger and the words smoother. And it’s jacked up the forgettability a few levels.

What’s missing is somebody whose actual job is to listen to how you talk about your business, find the parts that make it sound like yours, and put those parts on the page in a way that doesn’t bury your voice.

That’s what I do.

A quick word on AI.

Yes, AI can write copy. It can produce competent, professional, completely indistinguishable-from-everyone-else’s copy in about thirty seconds. If that’s what you’re after, you genuinely don’t need me — and I’m not being sarcastic. If your goal is just to get some words on your page or send out an email with some links, AI can check that box for you.

But if you want writing that sounds like your business has a personality worth paying attention to, well, that’s a different thing entirely. And that’s what I’m passionate about.

If you’ve read this far, you’re probably the right kind of person to work with.

Let’s start with an email. Tell me about your business, what you need written, and roughly when you need it. I’ll get back within a day or two with honest thoughts on whether I’m a good fit, plus a ballpark on cost and timeline.

If we’re moving forward, the next step is a real conversation. Not a sales call. Not a “let’s hop on a Zoom for fifteen minutes” that turns into ninety minutes. An actual conversation where I get to hear how you talk about your business, so I can write in a voice that sounds like yours — not a copywriter’s version of yours.

That conversation is where the writing actually starts.
