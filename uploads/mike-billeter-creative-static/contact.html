FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How long does a typical project take?
  • Website Copy Starter (3 pages): 2–3 weeks
  • Website Copy Standard (5 pages): 3–5 weeks
  • Website Copy Full Site (6+ pages): 5–6 weeks
  • Email Campaign: 1–2 weeks for a short sequence, 2–4 for a longer one
  • Blog Post or Article: 5–7 business days
  • Editing: usually within a week
  • Ghostwriting: varies by project
Can we start with something small before committing to a bigger project?

Yes. A small project is a low-risk way to see how we work together before committing to something bigger. Most of my ongoing clients started with something small and expanded from there.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Almost any industry. I've written for national food brands, private banks, universities, and small businesses in a dozen sectors. What actually matters is whether the business has a story and voice worth surfacing. Most do, whether they realize it yet or not.

What do you need from me to get started?

Not much. A conversation to talk through what you're trying to accomplish and how you'd describe your business in your own words. Brand guidelines, past copy, marketing collateral, and product information are helpful but not required. If you don't have that stuff, we'll figure it out together.

What if I don't like the first draft?

That's what the revision round is for. First drafts are a starting point, not a finished product. Tell me what's not landing and I'll rework it. If we still haven't nailed it after the included rounds, we keep going, billed hourly at that point.

Do you also do graphic design?

No. I've worked with dozens of graphic designers and appreciate what good design does for good writing, but I'm not a designer myself. If you need one, I'm happy to point you toward talented people I trust.

Are you accepting new clients right now?
