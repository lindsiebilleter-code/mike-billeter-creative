SERVICES.EXE

What I do, what it costs, and who it's actually for.

Prices are listed because I'd rather you self-select than spend a chunk of your life on a "let's get on a call to discuss pricing" that goes nowhere.

If you don't see what you're looking for, send me an email and ask. This covers maybe 90% of what people hire me for. The other 10% comes up in conversation.

Website Copy

Starts at $900

The thing most people hire me for. If your current website reads like every other website in your industry—or worse, like it was written by a committee that included a robot—this is the fix.

  • Starter — $900 — Up to three primary pages. One discovery conversation. Two rounds of revisions. A good fit for solo operators, consultants, and small businesses.
  • Standard — $1,350 — Up to five pages: three primary plus two secondary. Two rounds of revisions. A good fit for established small and mid-size businesses.
  • Full Site — $1,800+ — Six or more pages, scoped after we talk. Two rounds of revisions per page. A good fit for a rebrand or repositioning.

Email Campaigns

Starts at $650

Sequences that get opened, get read, and don't sound like they were written by a robot pretending to be a friendly human. Welcome, launch, nurture, and re-engagement sequences all welcome.

  • Up to 3 emails — $650
  • 4–5 emails — $950
  • 6–7 emails — $1,250
  • 8+ emails — scoped after we talk

Every email comes with a subject line, preview text, body copy, and call-to-action copy. I write the copy; I don't manage your email platform, set up automations, design templates, or guarantee open rates.

Editing

$150/hour, two-hour minimum

For businesses that already have copy they mostly like and want it sharper, tighter, and more distinctly theirs. This isn't a discount version of writing—it's a different service for a different situation.

I'll review what you have, edit for voice, clarity, and impact, and return a marked-up version with changes and notes. Substantive editing, not just proofreading. If what you need is a full rewrite, I'll tell you upfront.

Blog Posts & Articles

Starts at $300 per post

600–900 words of content that doesn't read like generic content. Longer pieces—1,200+ words, in-depth articles, and thought leadership—are scoped after we talk.

Best fit: businesses that want to publish consistently but don't have the time or inclination to write the posts themselves, and don't want to fill their site with the same SEO-stuffed slop everyone else is publishing.

Ghostwriting

$150/hour

LinkedIn posts, longer-form essays, op-eds, executive thought leadership—whatever you need written in your voice rather than mine.

The piece should sound like you wrote it on a particularly sharp day, not like someone else wrote it for you. I scope ghostwriting hourly because the time depends on how much I'm starting with and how much voice-capture the piece needs.

Ongoing Retainer

Starts at $450/month

For businesses that need writing regularly and would rather not start from scratch every time. Once we've worked together and I know your voice, a retainer is the most efficient way to keep me on call.

  • 3 hours/month — $450 — Light touch: a few small things per month.
  • 5 hours/month — $700 — Moderate: one bigger project plus some small stuff.
  • 8 hours/month — $1,050 — Heavy: a significant ongoing writing partnership.
  • Beyond 8 hours/month — Let's talk. Larger arrangements depend on availability.

Retainers are billed at the start of each month. Unused hours don't roll over. Either of us can end the arrangement with 30 days notice. I take on a small number at a time, and I'll tell you honestly if I'm at capacity.
