# PRODUCT.md — Mike Billeter Creative

## What this is
A portfolio and lead-generation site for Mike Billeter, a copywriter in Sioux Falls, South Dakota. Its one job: get the right kind of business owner to send Mike an email.

## Who it's for
Owners and marketing leads at small and mid-size businesses (and the occasional larger brand) who suspect their copy sounds like everyone else's and want a writer who will listen first. They value directness, published prices, and not being sold to.

## Core belief
Writing should sound like somebody wrote it. The site must demonstrate the belief, not describe it.

## Routes
- Home — the problem, the approach, services in brief, experience, working style, a word on AI, invitation to email
- Services — six services with full pricing, what Mike doesn't do, deposits/retainers/revisions/cancellation/ownership
- Process — six steps from first email to final invoice
- About — the cross-country walk, the work, philosophy, Sioux Falls life, faith, humor; testimonials gated until real ones exist
- Contact & FAQs — form (Name, Email, Project, Timeline, "Send It Over"), direct email fallback, seven FAQs

## Voice
Mike's copy is the product. It is used verbatim from the approved document. No buzzwords, no invented proof, no "elevate your brand."

## Secret
Typing `y2k` anywhere (outside a field) flips the whole site into a 1999 edition. Escape, or any "RETURN TO THE FUTURE" control, flips back. Mode persists for the browser session only.

## Success
A visitor understands what Mike does and what it costs within a minute, trusts the person behind it, and sends the first email.
